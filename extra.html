Device Information

WURFL Capabilities

WURFL helps developers learn information about the devices people use to visit a website.

1. brand_name

This capability identifies the brand of a device. For example, Apple or Samsung.

2. model_name

This capability identifies the model of a device. For example, a specific phone or tablet model.

3. device_os

This capability identifies the operating system of a device, such as Android or iOS.
